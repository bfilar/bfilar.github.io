Paper accepted at CAMLIS 2025: [*Evaluating LLM-Generated Detection Rules in Cybersecurity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16749) — an open-source benchmark and three metrics (detection accuracy, economic cost of syntactic correctness, robustness of query) for measuring LLM-generated security rules.
